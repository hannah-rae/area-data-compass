      About this prototype

      Built for the AI for Social Innovation conference (BYU) as a starting point for discussion with area leaders. Areas and 2025 priorities come from the Church's 2025 Caring for Those in Need report. Each area shows a sample of countries, not its full list.

      Live numbers on this page

      Rain, 3 months: UCSB Climate Hazards Center, CHIRPS3 Standardized Precipitation Index, 18-pentad accumulation (preliminary), averaged over each country. SPI below −1 is moderately dry or worse.

      Next 15 days: CHC CHIRPS-GEFS 15-day forecast anomaly (mm), run of .

       

      Nutrition, water, food insecurity: FAO, FAOSTAT Suite of Food Security Indicators (bulk download, retrieved ). FAO reports undernourishment below 2.5% as "<2.5".

      Coverage badges on data products are approximate in this mockup; confirm with each provider before relying on them.
