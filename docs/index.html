Area Data Compass Prototype

Each year, area leaders decide where humanitarian funds will do the most good. This page puts the open data on hunger, water, health, schooling, disasters, and poverty for your area next to where the Church's 2025 projects are, so you can see where needs are growing, whether current projects reach those places, and which data sources to follow during the year.

The numbers inform your judgment and local knowledge; they do not rank needs or recommend budgets. Data as of 21 October 2026.

  1. Choose your area and a topic. Topics with a ★ are your area's 2025 priorities. The topic stays at the top of the page and sets what every section shows.
  2. Compare countries. The map and table show the topic's key numbers for each country, with the year's projects on the map. Select a country.
  3. Look inside the country. Start with the key takeaways, then check provinces and districts against your projects, and find the data sources behind each number.

    About this prototype

    Built for the AI for Social Innovation conference (BYU). Project points come from the public layer behind the Caring Report 2025 interactive map (3,355 projects in 169 countries). Area priorities come from each area's page in the 2025 Caring Report. The layer has no area field, so countries are assigned to the report's eight areas by geography; check this list against the Church's own area boundaries.

    Project topics come from the map's project-type field (one type per project).

    Boundaries: geoBoundaries (CC BY 4.0) admin 1 and admin 2, simplified for display.

    Rain and food security

    Rain, last 3 months: UCSB Climate Hazards Center CHIRPS3 SPI, 18-pentad accumulation ending 15 Oct 2026 (preliminary), averaged over each district. SPI ≤ −1 is moderately dry or worse. During a region's dry season, small rain totals swing the SPI.

    Next 15 days: CHC CHIRPS-GEFS 15-day forecast anomaly (mm), run of 21 Oct 2026.

    Food insecurity outlook: FEWS NET, September 2026 outlook, projection for Oct 2026 – Jan 2027, IPC-compatible phases. Each district takes the phase of the FEWS NET unit at its center.

    Country numbers

    FAOSTAT Suite of Food Security Indicators; World Bank World Development Indicators (most recent value); INFORM Risk Index 2026 (EC JRC); UNHCR Refugee Population Statistics (2025); GDACS orange and red alerts, 21 Sep – 21 Oct 2026. All retrieved 21 Oct 2026.

    Coverage badges for sources not connected live are approximate.
